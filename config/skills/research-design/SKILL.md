---
name: research-design
description: Produce a conceptual design with decision-changing tests and explicit branches.
---
Compare at least two concrete alternatives against the stated constraints. Select conditionally or retain a no-go if a key premise is unsupported. Identify three to five decision-changing checks only where useful: what is measured, the necessary comparison, quality conditions, and support/reject/inconclusive branches. Match each check to its inference level; expression, activity, causal dependence and functional benefit are distinct. For nonbiological questions use the corresponding observable system outcomes. State what would change the selected design. Do not invent parameters, measurements, completed experiments or operational biological recipes.

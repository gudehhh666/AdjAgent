---
name: research-synthesis
description: Turn retrieved evidence into a concise answer with explicit comparison and uncertainty.
---
Answer the requested question first. Compare alternatives using the same criteria and the actual requested comparator. Separate observations, interpretations and recommendations. Cite each material externally sourced claim to content actually read; keep citations next to the claim. Explain what evidence changed the decision or confidence, without claiming a causal experimental result. Preserve missing evidence as a bounded limitation. Do not replace the endpoint with a broader proxy or crowd out the answer with a search diary. If sources disagree, explain the relevant difference in setting or method.

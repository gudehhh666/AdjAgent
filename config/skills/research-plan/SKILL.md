---
name: research-plan
description: Decompose an open research question into decision-relevant evidence needs.
---
State the requested decision, alternatives, endpoint and comparison. Separate known inputs from assumptions. Identify at most three unresolved facts that could change the answer. Choose sources by the kind of evidence needed, using the resource catalog when useful. Do not visit every database or infer a required source from a benchmark label. Reserve synthesis time. Stop a local search after two distinct attempts add no useful evidence; record that gap without treating it as absence. A short public plan is enough; no private reasoning trace is required.

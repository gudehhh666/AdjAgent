---
name: research-evidence
description: Check source identity, relevance and scope before using a claim.
---
Match entity, study, setting, endpoint and comparator. Read exact fields or passages, retaining source ID, selector and scope. Metadata locates a study; it cannot supply its missing observations. Methods describe measurements, not their outcomes. A source's existence or literal quote does not prove the proposed interpretation. Look for a relevant alternative or counterexample. Use specific comparable evidence; do not combine incompatible numerical results. Preserve unavailable/full-text/figure limitations and do not fill them from memory as if observed. Carry exact source locators into synthesis.
